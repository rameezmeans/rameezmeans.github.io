How I Designed a Credit, Payment and Invoice System for a SaaS Platform at Scale

When payment is added to a SaaS product, the gateway is only one small part of the work. In this system, payments, credits, services, invoices and different portal rules had to work together for a platform serving more than 15,000 businesses.

I wrote about why I kept these parts separate, how the same backend supports different portals and payment gateways, and why a failed invoice should not undo a successful payment. The main lesson is simple: automation is not only removing manual work. You also have to design how the system recovers when something fails.
